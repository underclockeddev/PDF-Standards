PDF/A, and which version a judicial agency should use

A five-minute read. Every factual statement carries a numbered citation; click it to jump to the source.

Our recommendation
PDF/A-2u default

PDF/A-2a when your tools produce properly tagged (structured) documents. PDF/A-2b for scanned paper. When a court's e-filing system names a version, use the version it names.

This recommendation is our analysis of the cited facts below, not a statement taken from any one source. See the reasoning →

PDF/A in three lines

  • 1It is an ISO standard (ISO 19005) for long-term preservation of electronic documents.1
  • 2Its goal is to keep a document's static visual appearance over time, independent of the tools used to create, store or render it.5
  • 3It works by requiring that everything needed to render the file (fonts, colour profiles, images) is inside the file.11

PDF/A compared with the other PDF standards

"PDF" is one format with several ISO-standardized subsets. Each subset is built for a different job. PDF/A is the only one built for archiving.

ISO 32000

PDF (base format)

The base specification. PDF/A-1 builds on PDF 1.4, PDF/A-2 and -3 on PDF 1.7 (ISO 32000-1), and PDF/A-4 on PDF 2.0 (ISO 32000-2).86

ISO 19005

PDF/A, for archiving

Long-term preservation. Built to maximize device independence, self-containment and self-documentation.5

ISO 14289

PDF/UA, for accessibility

Universal Accessibility. Supports assistive technology such as screen readers. First published in 2012.1213

ISO 15930

PDF/X, for printing

Graphic arts and professional print workflows. Strict subsets of PDF for accurate print reproduction.12

ISO 24517

PDF/E, for engineering

Engineering documents, including interactive 3D. Published in 2008.12

ISO 16612

PDF/VT, for variable data printing

High-speed variable-data and transactional printing. Builds on PDF/X.12

PDF/A and PDF/UA are separate standards. PDF/A preserves how a document looks. PDF/UA makes it usable with assistive technology.12 The Library of Congress lists both among its recommended formats for digital textual works.16

The four parts of PDF/A

Each part is a separate ISO standard, and a newer part does not replace an older one. The number after "PDF/A-" is the part; the letter after it is the conformance level.

Conformance levels (the letter)

The levels apply to PDF/A-1, -2 and -3. They set how much a file guarantees beyond looking correct.

b

Basic: looks right

Covers the visual appearance of the document, but not its structural or semantic properties.7 It is the level typically used for files made from scanned pages.7

Appearance only. Text extraction not guaranteed.9
u

Unicode: looks right and text is reliable

Everything in level b, plus every character maps to a Unicode value, so text can be reliably searched and extracted.39 Introduced with PDF/A-2.3

Appearance plus searchable text
a

Accessible: looks right, reliable text, and logical structure

Requires tagged structure (headings, lists, tables, reading order), a language setting and Unicode mappings.10 Takes more effort to produce.10

Appearance, text and structure
"b" is the most common trap. A level-b file can look perfect and still be unreadable by machines: level b does not require the font mapping that turns glyphs back into characters.9 That affects search, e-discovery and copy/paste.

Recommendation for a slow-moving judicial agency

Your agency changes systems slowly, keeps records for decades, and its documents must stay readable and searchable. Answer the questions below in order.

⚖️
Are you filing into a court e-filing system that names a PDF/A version?
That court's rule wins. Federal court PDF/A FAQs for CM/ECF filers: PDF/A-1a preferred, PDF/A-1b the minimum. PDF/A-1a requires structure and suits electronic documents; PDF/A-1b suits scans.1718
Follow the court
📝
Is the document born-digital (Word, case management system, etc.)?
Choose level u so the text stays searchable.9 Choose level a if your tools produce tagged structure.10 Federal courts advise creating PDF/A from the word processor, not by printing and scanning.19
PDF/A-2u (or 2a)
🖨️
Is it scanned paper?
Level b is the level typically used for scanned pages.7 On its own, level b does not guarantee searchable text.9
PDF/A-2b
📎
Do you need to embed spreadsheets, audio, source files or other non-PDF files?
NARA says not to use the attachments feature of PDF/A-3 or PDF/A-4.15 Keep those files as separate records instead.
Avoid PDF/A-3 / 4f

Why PDF/A-2 over the other parts

✓
Federal archival guidance favors it. NARA lists PDF/A-1 and PDF/A-2 as preferred formats for textual content and does not list PDF/A-3.14 PDF/A-compliant filings also make it easier for the federal judiciary to archive with NARA.17
✓
It handles what modern office software produces. PDF/A-2 allows transparency, layers and JPEG 2000. PDF/A-1 does not.8
✓
It has the "u" level. Level u guarantees searchable text without the extra work of full tagging.39 PDF/A-1 has no "u" level.3
✕
PDF/A-3 makes the archive responsible for every embedded file. An institution may end up maintaining formats it never meant to preserve, and must check that embedded files are not infected with viruses. The standard also gives no straightforward way to tell whether a file has attachments.14
!
PDF/A-4 is newer than its software support. PDF 2.0, the base of PDF/A-4, has not been widely adopted, and the Library of Congress found no software that declares itself a conforming PDF/A-4 processor.6 An agency that changes slowly benefits from mature, widely supported tools.
Planning to follow the federal courts' PDF/A-1 guidance? PDF/A-1 is also NARA-preferred.14 The trade-off: PDF/A-1 does not allow transparency or layers, and it has no "u" level.83

Notable limitations

What PDF/A does not do, and what it rules out.

🔒

No encryption or passwords

PDF/A does not allow encryption.5 Access control has to come from your records system, not from the file.

🛡️

Not proof of authenticity

PDF/A is a file format and gives no special protection against data corruption. On its own it does not guarantee long-term preservation.11

🎞️

No audio or video

Audio and video content are forbidden in PDF/A-1, -2 and -3.5

⚙️

No scripts or dynamic behavior

JavaScript and executable file launches are prohibited in PDF/A-1, -2 and -3.5 Forms that rely on scripts will not carry their logic over.

🔤

Fonts must be embeddable

All fonts must be embedded. Some copyrighted fonts cannot be legally embedded.11

🔁

Converting old PDFs can lose features

Most PDFs can be converted, but some features may not carry over to PDF/A.11

♿

PDF/A alone is not accessibility compliance

Accessibility is a separate standard, PDF/UA (ISO 14289).13 Level "a" adds tagging, but the PDF Association publishes separate guidance on meeting both PDF/A and PDF/UA.1021

✅

"Saved as PDF/A" ≠ valid PDF/A

Check files with a validator. veraPDF is a free, open-source validator for all PDF/A parts and levels, developed under the PDF Association and maintained by the Open Preservation Foundation.20
